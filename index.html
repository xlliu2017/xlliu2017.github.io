About

I am an Associate Professor at Ocean University of China. My research develops mathematical and computational tools for scientific machine learning, with an emphasis on neural operators, neural-network theory, and efficient solvers for multiscale partial differential equations.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in Computer, Electrical and Mathematical Science and Engineering at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) (2022–2025), working with Prof. Jinchao Xu. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2021 under the supervision of Prof. Lei Zhang, and was a visiting scholar at Caltech with Prof. Houman Owhadi in 2019–2020.

Research Interests

I develop AI-driven numerical methods for scientific computing, especially for multiscale problems and partial differential equations. My work connects approximation theory and numerical analysis with modern machine learning.

  • Neural-network theory, kernels, and approximation
  • Operator learning and AI for PDEs
  • Multiscale, multigrid, and numerical homogenization methods
  • Graph learning and scientific machine learning

My work has appeared in Journal of Computational Physics, SIAM Multiscale Modeling & Simulation, Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis, NeurIPS, ICLR, and LoG.

Diff-ANO

Operator learning · Multiscale PDEs

Self-composing neural operators

Build a deeper PDE surrogate by reusing one backbone with shared weights. Train-and-Unroll grows the composition depth during training, without adding new backbone parameters.

Paper
Nonlinear PDEs · AI for science

Newton-informed neural operator

Learn the Newton correction rather than the final solution: predict an update, add it to the current iterate, and repeat. Training uses the linearized PDE equation.

Paper

Selected papers

HANO: Mitigating spectral bias

Hierarchical attention connects local detail with long-range interactions. An empirical H1 loss helps learn high-frequency components of multiscale PDE solutions.

Journal of Computational Physics, 2024

Paper

ACMP: Allen–Cahn message passing

Model node features as interacting particles: attraction and repulsion maintain separation, while an Allen–Cahn reaction stabilizes the dynamics. This counters oversmoothing in deep graph networks.

ICLR 2023 · Spotlight

Paper Figure 1

MgNO: Neural operators via multigrid

Parameterize linear operators with a learnable multigrid V-cycle. Fine-grid smoothing and coarse-grid corrections connect local structure with long-range coupling.

ICLR 2024

Paper Code

Sobolev spaces meet linearized ReLU networks

An integral representation connects Sobolev regularity to ReLUk ridge functions. For sufficiently smooth targets and suitably distributed inner parameters, fitting only linear coefficients achieves optimal approximation rates up to logarithmic factors.

Mathematics of Computation · Accepted
